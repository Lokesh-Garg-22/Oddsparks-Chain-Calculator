## What we changed

- Extracted recipe and building data from the wiki's `{{Recipe}}` and `{{Infobox building}}` templates.
- Normalised field and item names, merged duplicate recipes, and recorded the source page and revision of each recipe.
- Added corrections, in-game display names and confirmed values in `overrides.json`. Each comes with its own source.
