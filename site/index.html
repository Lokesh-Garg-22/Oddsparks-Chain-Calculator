Oddsparks Chain Calculator

The calculator is being built

Pick a building, choose a recipe and say how many you want to run. The calculator will list every other building and raw resource needed to keep them supplied, with spark crews, saved tabs and Excel export.

Game data comes from the Oddsparks Wiki and is being checked against the game.
