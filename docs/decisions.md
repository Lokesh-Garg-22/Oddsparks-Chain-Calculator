- **Step 10 (saved library) decisions:**
  - **Layout, as in the mockup:** the library is a column beside the calculator, opened with Library in the header. On screens up to 900 px wide it's a drawer over the page, which closes after opening a tab.
  - **The chain tree measures its own width** (a CSS container query): below 720 px it switches to its compact cards. That covers phones and the narrower results column when the library is open, with one rule.
  - **Storage format:** each folder lists its children in order (subfolders and tabs mixed), and `root` lists the top level. This replaces the planned `parentId`/`tabIds`/`order`/`unfiled` fields, because one ordered list per folder makes drag and drop and "Move to…" simple and leaves nothing to keep in step. `cleanLibrary()` repairs a damaged or hand-edited library: duplicates, loops and orphans. storage-and-export.md is updated.

  - **Restore asks** to add the backup to the library (clashing ids get new ones) or replace it. A file that isn't a backup is refused with a message. Restoring into an empty library doesn't ask.
  - **Asking before the browser tab closes (`beforeunload`)** is on whenever an open tab has unsaved changes.
  - **Storage blocked:** the library still works for the visit, says it will be lost, and back up still works.
  - **Found while testing:** menus near the bottom of the screen ran off it (for example the Columns menu, once the toolbar pushed it down). They now open upward or cap their height when there isn't room below.

- **Step 5 (calculation engine) decisions:**
  - **Approach:** a depth-first walk from the target fixes the dependency order and cuts loops on the path. Rates are then computed consumers-first, so demand from all branches is summed before a recipe runs (no double counting, one row per recipe).
  - **A recipe in the chain for several of its outputs** runs for the most demanding one; the rest are byproducts. Byproducts don't reduce other demand (v1, as decided).
  - **Missing values don't crash:** a recipe with no craft time gives `machines: null` and a warning. Today's data has none.
  - **Saved recipe ids are resolved before the engine** (`resolveId`). An unknown recipe pick, or one that doesn't make the item, falls back to the default.
  - **After the `simplify` review, the result is a graph:**
    - every input names its source row or why the chain stops; every output says how much is used and what's left
    - per-craft amounts are included, so Excel formulas share the engine's cycles and odds rules
    - the tree is derived from the rows, and the rate formulas exist once
    - warnings are objects, and the engine takes a tab's stored `calc` as is
    - measured: the largest real chain (28 rows) takes about 0.1 ms
  - **Checks:** the owner's 3 spreadsheets, their in-game readings and the Logger cross-check are tests against the engine itself. Sheet "Wooden Panel" gives exactly 4 Sawn Timber Sawbenches, where the sheet had 4.02 from using the rounded 11.2.

- **Step 2 (wiki extractor) decisions:**
  - **Merge rules:**
    - only values a page states are compared
    - most pages win, a tie goes to the building's own page, fuel any-yes
    - two blocks on one page are always two recipes
    - name variants of up to 3 letters merge if the numbers agree

    Reason: the first version let the building page always win, which would have picked the Loom page's "824 s" typo for Sponge over three pages saying 24 s.
  - **Data stores facts, not a status:**
    - `sources` with revisions, `valueFrom`, `conflicts` by field path, `problems[]`
    - flags nobody states stay `null`
    - temperature split into `optimalTemperature` (a number) and `insulated`

    Reason: overrides (step 4) need to address individual fields, and the app works out a status after applying them. Recorded after the `simplify` review.
  - **Recipe IDs depend only on content** (building + main product, plus inputs, name or hash only when needed). Reason: saved tabs store IDs. Order-based `-2` suffixes could have silently swapped recipes when someone reordered a wiki page.
  - **`dataVersion` includes a content hash.** The hard-coded game version is dropped.
  - **New override key `mergeRecipes`** for "these two extracted recipes are one". `renamedIds` stays for moving saved-tab IDs.
  - **The extractor keeps a raw wikitext snapshot** in `tools/.cache/` (git-ignored), so `npm run extract -- --offline` rebuilds without touching the wiki. A live run makes about 6 requests, one at a time, with a 400 ms pause between them.
  - **Security review:** no findings. Hardening applied anyway: wiki text is escaped in the report, and maps keyed by wiki text have no inherited keys.
